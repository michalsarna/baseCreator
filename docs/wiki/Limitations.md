# Limitations

* **No machine:** no devices, connections, G-code, preview of a job or camera. Use LightCreator, MillCreator or PenCreator for that.
* **Flat drawings only.** The 3D model and the relief STL are in MillCreator.
* **Pictures are not exported.** SVG, PLT and DXF hold outlines; trace a picture first.
* **UGS designs** bring shapes on layers; feeds and depths are ignored.
* **The PLT file** has been checked by tests of its text, not on a real plotter or in other programs.
* **Not signed by a paid developer account:** macOS and Windows warn the first time. See [Installation](Installation).
* **Page setup** has no bleed or print settings, and the program does not print.
