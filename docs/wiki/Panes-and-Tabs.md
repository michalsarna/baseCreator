# Panes and Tabs

How to arrange the work area: two drawings at once, one above the other or side by side. Panes work as in the other programs; here every tab is a drawing, as there is no preview, G-code or camera.

## Tabs

Every open drawing is a **tab** in a bar above the work area, with a dot when it has unsaved changes. At most 10 drawings are open at once. The **+** at the end of a bar makes a new drawing. Opening a project that is open already shows its tab.

A **pane** is a part of the work area with its own bar of tabs. There are **three panes at most**.

## Make more panes

Open at least **two tabs** first. Then either:

* **View → Split view: side by side** or **View → Split view: one above the other**: the last tab of the bar goes to a new pane; or
* right-click a tab and choose **Move to pane 2** (or **3**, from a pane with two tabs or more).

## Move a tab

**Drag the tab** onto the bar or the body of another pane, or along its own bar to change the order. Or use its menu (right click). Dragging moves a tab only between the panes there are: a new pane comes from the menu.

## Sizes and direction

* **Drag the line between two panes** to change their sizes; **double-click it** to make them equal.
* **View → Split view: …** changes the direction when there are two or three panes.

## Back to one pane

**View → Single view** puts all the tabs in one pane. Closing or moving the last tab of a pane closes the pane.

## Close a tab

Click its cross, click it with the **middle button**, or choose **Close tab** in its menu. A drawing with unsaved changes asks first.

## Which drawing the tools work on

One drawing is the **active** one: the tools, the menus and the keys work on it. A drawing in another pane is shown for looking only, with a zoom of its own. **Click it, or its tab, to make it the active one.**

## The right panel

The panel at the right is separate from the panes. Its tabs are **Properties**, **Layers**, **Clipart** and **Description**.

* **Drag a tab** to change the order. **Drag the left edge** of the panel to change its width.
* The arrow at the start of the row **hides the panel**: the names of the tabs stay on a narrow strip. Click a name to open the panel on that tab.

## Remembered

The panes, the tabs in them, the direction, the sizes, the order of the right panel's tabs and whether it is hidden come back when you start the program again.
